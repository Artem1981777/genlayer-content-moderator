How it works
  1. Create an item with moderation rules.
  2. Ingest a source URL (author stakes).
  3. Report suspicious content (reporter bonds).
  4. Moderate — the AI scores 7 axes and returns a verdict.
  5. Enforce — stakes are settled into the payout ledger.
  6. Appeal and Resolve if the author disputes.

About

ContentModerator Registry v1.0.0 — a multi-item, staking-based content-moderation protocol on GenLayer Bradbury. Nondeterministic AI scoring runs inside the Intelligent Contract under gl.eq_principle for validator consensus.

7 axes: scam · spam · harassment · hate · violence · sexual · self_harm. Verdicts: APPROVE (below 50) · FLAG (50–79) · REMOVE (80+). Confidence self-calibrates from the top axis score.
