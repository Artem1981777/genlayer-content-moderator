About
Multi-item content-moderation registry on GenLayer Bradbury. An LLM Intelligent Contract scores content across 7 axes with self-calibrating confidence and auto-escalation; authors stake, reporters bond, and payouts settle on-chain with a transparent ledger. Appeals are contestable and resolved on-chain. All reads and writes are live from your wallet.
